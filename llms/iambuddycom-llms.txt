# Buddy (iambuddy.com / buddy.insure)

> Buddy is a digital insurance commerce platform that turns insurance product rules, pricing, and workflows into structured data via its Insurance Object Notation (ION) engine, so carriers, MGAs, brokers, and agents can sell P&C insurance online across direct, agent-assisted, embedded, and agentic channels. Its Offer Element embeddable checkout adds quote/apply/bind/pay in a few lines of code, and Buddy MCP connects insurance products to AI assistants for conversational selling. SOC 2 Type 2 and PCI DSS compliant; licensed in all 50 states.

## Products
- [Solutions](https://buddy.insure/solutions): Carriers, MGAs, and Brokers & Agents solutions built on the ION engine.
- [Offer Element](https://buddy.insure/buddy-blog/why-insurance-uis-are-hard-and-how-we-fixed-it): Embeddable insurance checkout widget (quote, apply, bind, pay).
- [Buddy MCP](https://buddy.insure/buddy-blog/introduction-buddy-mcp): Model Context Protocol server exposing query, quote, bind, and pay over indexed insurance products (early access).
